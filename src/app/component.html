🔮 Next Arcana

Welcome to Next Arcana — a reflective tool that helps you explore possible futures through honest self-reflection, the symbolic cycles of tarot, and a touch of AI interpretation.

How does it work?
  1. Choose what you want to reflect on

    Think about a person, situation, emotion, relationship, project, or any state of mind you want to understand better.

    Example: “I just came back from work, and I still can't mentally disconnect from it.”

  2. Describe that state using tarot cards

    Select cards that symbolically represent your current feelings, thoughts, or situation.

    Example: “I feel emotionally detached from others. My only focus was delivering the best possible product. I still can't stop thinking about the project.”

  3. Provide additional context

    Press the Predict button and describe the circumstances surrounding your reflection.

    Example: “I had a presentation for my school project, and I feel like my work stood out from everyone else's.”

  4. Receive your Arcana

    The AI will interpret your symbolic choices and generate a reflective prediction — not as absolute truth, but as a mirror for possibility, emotion, and direction.
